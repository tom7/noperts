shadowanim: almost passing through itself (the canyon)
=====================================================

What it shows
-------------
A polyhedron is Rupert if a copy of it can pass through a hole in it: seen
from some direction, the shadow of a slightly turned copy fits strictly
inside the shadow of the original. The animation shows the two shadows for
this branch's solid:

- the original's shadow, filled (blue-grey);
- the turned copy's shadow, outlined (white): the copy is rotated by a tiny
  angle (1e-7 radians) about the axis that helps it most, then slid sideways
  as well as possible (an exact linear program);
- in red, wherever the copy's shadow sticks out of the original's.

At this angle the two shadows coincide to the eye, so the left half shows
the whole shadow and the right half three magnified insets, centered on the
three places where the copy sticks out most (circled on the left). The
magnification is the same in every frame, so the size of the red is
comparable across the whole animation.

Phase 1 (12 s). The viewing direction travels from an ordinary view to the
"canyon": the direction from which the solid comes closest to passing
through itself (the smallest first-order rigidity margin, found by sampling
all views and refining). The approach slows down exponentially near the
canyon. The red shrinks: at the canyon, the copy sticks out by only (angle x
margin).

Phase 2 (8 s). The view stays at the canyon, and the axis of the copy's
rotation swings away from the best one and back. The red grows again: only
rotations near that one axis come close to fitting.

What it means for the proof
---------------------------
The red never disappears: to first order, every small rotation of the copy
sticks out somewhere, by at least (angle x margin), and the margin is
positive for every view. The proof's "identity tube" turns this into a
certificate: near the identity rotation, contact points like the circled
ones block every small rotation, with an explicit radius. Views with a small
margin are where the certificates are thinnest. viewmap's --margin output
maps the margin over all views (and lists the canyon with the object
rotation that shows it in Blender).

Usage
-----
./shadowanim.exe --out canyon.mov [--width 3840 --height 2160]
  [--seconds1 12 --seconds2 8] [--theta 1e-7] [--canyon x,y,z] [--start x,y,z]
Without --canyon, the canyon is found automatically (it prints it). Frames
are lossless PNG in a .mov at 60 Hz (codec/mov.h).
